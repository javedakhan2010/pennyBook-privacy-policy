Privacy Policy

Personal Finance, Expense Tracking, Budgeting & Udhaar Ledger

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 Application: PennyBook (com.pennybook.expensetracker) Developer: Javed Khan

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: Local-First Financial Privacy

Your financial transactions, budgets, investments, and personal debt/credit (Udhaar) records are stored strictly on your physical device in a private SQLite database. PennyBook does not upload, sync, or transmit your financial ledger to any external server or cloud database.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how PennyBook ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Javed Khan, manages data when you use the Android application. We are committed to safeguarding your personal financial privacy and providing transparent, clear disclosures regarding data storage, optional services, and device permissions.

2. Local-First Financial Data Storage

PennyBook is engineered with a client-side, local-first architecture:

  • On-Device SQLite Database: All financial entries—including income, expense transactions, category assignments, payment methods, monthly budgets, investment records, and friendly loans/Udhaar ledgers—are stored strictly on your local device storage via Android Room.
  • No Financial Cloud Sync: We do not operate external financial servers or databases. Your personal financial ledger remains exclusively in your possession.
  • No Mandatory Account Registration: You are not required to create an account, log in with an email, or provide government/bank identification to use PennyBook.

Decoupled Isolation: Network requests performed by advertising or Google Play Billing SDKs operate in complete isolation from the local Room database. Your financial records are never bundled with advertising identifiers or transmitted over the network.

4. Android Device Permissions

PennyBook requests only standard Android permissions necessary to deliver user-requested functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used to deliver local notifications for payment due dates, bank loan EMI reminders, and daily scheduled auto-backup completion.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to schedule timely notifications for debt due dates and automated daily database backups at the user's configured hour.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to reschedule active reminder alarms after your device restarts.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Utilized by Google Play Billing and advertising partner SDKs for purchase verification and ad delivery. Never used to upload financial data.
  • Storage Access (SAF / External Storage): Used solely to allow you to save database backups, export reports (PDF, CSV, Excel), or restore backups from your device's Documents/PennyBook folder.

5. Data Ownership, Backup & Deletion

You maintain 100% ownership and physical custody of your financial records:

  • Database Backup & Portability: You can create encrypted database backups or export your complete financial records into CSV, Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`), or PDF format at any time.
  • One-Tap Data Deletion: You can permanently erase all transactions, accounts, and settings at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Reset & Clear All Data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling PennyBook immediately removes its internal private database sandbox from your device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PennyBook is a general audience personal finance application and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

7. Security of Your Information

Because your financial ledger resides on your device, its physical security is tied to your phone's native security features. We recommend protecting your device with a secure lock screen PIN, password, or biometric authentication.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements, service updates, or regulatory adjustments. Any revisions will be published on this webpage with an updated "Effective Date".
